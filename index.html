OpenAI

The State of Enterprise AI

The deepening use of enterprise AI and its impact on productivity and business outcomes.

Executive Summary

Enterprise AI is moving from an experimental tool to core business infrastructure. As usage grows, AI is becoming more deeply embedded in daily workflows, with companies reporting measurable results in productivity, customer experience, and product development.

Critical Takeaways

  • ChatGPT Enterprise message volume increased 8x year over year.
  • API reasoning-token consumption per organization increased 320x.
  • Users report saving approximately 40–60 minutes per day.
  • Value is shifting from simple assistant use toward AI embedded directly into workflows.

Accenture

The New Rules of Platform Strategy in the Age of Agentic AI

Joint redesign of platform, AI, and business strategy.

Executive Summary

Agentic AI is becoming a new orchestration layer across systems rather than merely a feature added to existing platforms. Scalable value requires AI, platform, and business strategies to be aligned under a common architecture and operating model.

Critical Takeaways

  • 94% of leaders expect platform strategy to change, while 57% believe it requires reinvention.
  • Companies aligning AI, platform, and business strategy report an average 2.2x revenue growth and a 37% increase in EBITDA.
  • Agentic architecture requires data access, governance, and platform integration to be designed together.
  • Strategic silos are a major reason pilots fail to scale.

Agents moving into production, ROI, use cases, and scaling practices.

BCG + MIT SMR

Managing the Machines That Manage Themselves

Managing agentic AI as both software and a digital coworker.

Executive Summary

Agentic AI creates a new category between traditional software management and people management. Creating value requires processes, decision rights, governance, roles, and investment models to be redesigned with an agentic-first approach.

Critical Takeaways

  • 35% of companies are using agentic AI, while 44% plan to adopt it soon.
  • 47% still do not have a clear AI strategy.
  • Four core tensions: scalability vs. adaptability, investment vs. employment, oversight vs. autonomy, and retrofit vs. redesign.
  • Leading companies are making governance and decision rights more dynamic.

Bain & Company

Building the Foundation for Agentic AI

Technology foundations and architectural readiness for agentic AI.

Executive Summary

Unlike standalone productivity tools, agentic AI requires structural changes to enterprise technology architecture. Success depends on agents being able to work reliably across data, application, security, and process layers.

Critical Takeaways

  • End-to-end process and architecture design is required instead of a fragmented copilot approach.
  • Identity, authorization, data access, observability, and security are foundational capabilities.
  • Platform standardization is critical to scaling without increasing technical debt.

McKinsey

The Agentic Organization: Contours of the Next Paradigm for the AI Era

Five foundational pillars of a new organizational model where humans and AI agents work together.

Executive Summary

McKinsey frames agentic transformation not as a technology deployment but as a shift in organizational paradigm. The emerging model is more horizontal and AI-first, with small outcome-oriented human teams directing large numbers of specialized agents.

Critical Takeaways

  • Five pillars: business model, operating model, governance, people/culture, and technology/data.
  • Models in which teams of 2–5 people manage 50–100 specialized agents are already emerging.
  • Governance must become real-time, embedded, and data-driven, while ultimate accountability remains human.
  • Outcome-oriented networks of agentic teams may begin to replace functional silos.
  • Transformation should be designed by senior leadership using a future-back approach.
